# The key ring: prompts for "Go Live Please"

Christian's idea (Oct 19, 2026): every digital key the site runs on, made real. High-tech keys glowing in the site's
turquoise, hung on the belt of an old-school robot janitor. We only ever see the belt and the key holder, never the
whole robot.

**One house rule shapes these:** no brands, logos or text inside an AI render (CLAUDE.md), and generators garble
words anyway. So each key carries a **symbol** for what it opens instead of a name or a logo. The names go on the page
beside the picture (captions, or a small legend like the one at the bottom), where they stay sharp and can be changed.

Every prompt ends with the same guard line; keep it.

---

## The look (paste at the start of any prompt below)

> The belt and key ring of an old-school robot janitor, seen up close in real space: a wide, scuffed leather work belt
> with brass rivets over a dented, olive-green enameled steel body with chipped paint, rounded 1950s robot panels,
> a coiled retractable key reel and a heavy brass ring. On the ring hang high-tech keys: slim translucent glass and
> brushed-steel keys with glowing cores in the site's turquoise, from pale aqua through bright turquoise to deep
> teal (#7ff5e8, #25c0ad, #00b3a4, #0f6f6a), each key's bow holding one simple glowing symbol etched in light.
> Warm tungsten workshop light against the cool turquoise glow, shallow depth of field, photographic, real materials,
> fine dust in the air.

**Guard line (end of every prompt):**
> No text, letters, numbers, words, logos or brand marks anywhere in the image; symbols only.

## 1. The hero shot (cover)

> [The look.] A close, low three-quarter view of the robot janitor's hip: the belt fills the frame from edge to edge,
> the key ring hangs from a brass clip at the center, and seven high-tech keys fan out and catch the light, each with
> its own glowing symbol: a door with a padlock, three small tags, a branching tree with a tiny hourglass charm, a
> speech bubble with a spark, a cloud with a downward arrow and an eye, a hand offering a folded page, and a heart-
> shaped keyhole. One empty brass hook hangs beside them. Behind, out of focus: a workshop corridor, a mop bucket, a
> row of server cabinets with small turquoise status lights. [Guard line.]

Formats: 16:9 for the Note's cover; 3:4 for a magazine cover crop (keep the keys in the middle two-thirds).

## 2. The loop (cover video)

> [The look.] The same belt and key ring. The robot takes two slow steps and stops; the keys swing and jingle, settle,
> and their glowing cores pulse once in turn from left to right, the heart keyhole last and brightest. Locked-off
> camera, 6 to 8 seconds, seamless loop, the last frame matching the first. [Guard line.]

## 3. Close-ups (one per key, for the steps and the glossary)

Use one for each key; swap in its symbol and glow from the table.

> [The look.] An extreme close-up of one high-tech key hanging from the janitor's brass ring, the belt leather soft
> in the background. The key is translucent glass over brushed steel, its core glowing [glow], and in its bow one
> simple symbol etched in light: [symbol]. Fingerprints and fine scratches on the steel, a drop of light on the glass
> edge. [Guard line.]

Suggested order on the page: 1 deploy key, 2 the three tags, 3 GitHub key, 6 pull-request key, 7 Studio key,
4 drafting key, 5 Drive key, then the empty hook.

## 4. The empty hook (the closing image)

> [The look.] Macro shot of the brass key ring with the keys pushed to one side, and one empty hook in sharp focus at
> the center, catching a thin line of turquoise light reflected from the keys beside it. Quiet, still, a little
> wry. [Guard line.]

## 5. The handover (optional, for the client idea)

> [The look.] A brass robot hand unclipping a second, smaller key ring from the janitor's belt and holding it out
> toward the camera: the same kind of glowing keys, fewer of them, newly cut and bright, with a blank brass tag.
> [Guard line.]

This is the "a client could run their site the same way" picture: a set of keys cut for someone else.
