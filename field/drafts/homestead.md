# The Lake Home: the homestead below the lake

Christian's idea (Oct 19, 2026): the home page is the lake, and below the lake is a homestead, a small world where
every part of the site is a place. Made with care and attention to detail, full of tiny hidden easter eggs: "enjoying
the journey through the world one little pixel at a time."

Nothing here is built yet. This is the plan to mark up; the first map is built from it. The art: field/drafts/homestead-prompts.md.

## How it works

- **The lake stays on top.** Scroll past the shore and the path leads down into the homestead.
- **It follows the lake.** Same season (snow on the roofs in winter, leaves in fall), same night (windows light up,
  easing on).
- **Every place is a door and a filter.** Hover: a soft glow, a small lift, its window lamps ease on, its name shows as
  text on the page (never inside a picture). Click: go to that section, or show the Notes of that kind.
- **On a phone** the places come one at a time down the hillside, like walking down a path.
- **Drawn in code**, in the lake's flat vector style, so seasons, night and the lights come almost free and the page
  stays fast. AI paintings can come later for the insides of buildings.
- **Motion rule (CLAUDE.md):** everything eases, unhurried; reduced motion shows things at once.

### The Studio: VIP only (Christian, Oct 19)

A small Studio on the grounds, so anyone with a login gets to /studio/ straight from the site.

- **The VIP door.** Signed in (the Studio's own sign-in, kept 30 days): the door's lamp is lit and it opens straight
  into the Studio. Not signed in: the door asks for the sign-in; visitors never see anything private, only the door.
  The check is a yes or no from the Studio, nothing more (no names, no keys in the page).
- **The light show, for everyone.** Through the big window anyone can watch the coloured liquid flow through the
  Studio's glass pipes and heart, using the Studio's own river pictures and clips (studio/table/a, blue, green and
  orange, by day and by night), easing from one colour to the next.
- **The colour follows the time of day,** the way Christian sets his: blue in the morning, green in the daytime,
  orange at night. In the homestead it simply follows the visitor's clock.
- **In the Studio itself (built, Oct 19):** the Studio follows the clock too (studio/table/clock.js): blue mornings
  (6 am to noon), green days (noon to 7 pm), orange nights (7 pm to 6 am). Throwing either switch takes over by hand,
  to test and see; the HOME TIME plate beside them, its lamp lit while the clock is in charge, hands it back.

### The Automaton Labs, on stilts by the water

Glass-and-brass sheds on a dock. Notes: science, tech, AI (Popular Paradox, HEATSINK, HIDDEN LAYER).

- The water wheel: turned by the stream, it lights the labs.
- The lighthouse at the end of the dock: gear-driven, a slow beam at night that points at the latest Note.
- The test pool: a little diving robot or a toy boat being tried out.

### A.L.T.A.'s tubes (Christian, Oct 19)

Big glass tubes rising out of the green grass just below the lake, banded in brass, full of lake water, curving over
toward the lake at the top. A.L.T.A. is seen coming and going through them: now and then she rises up a tube, pauses at
a porthole, and is gone over the top toward the lake, or comes back down. The lake scene itself isn't touched. Her
own cut-out clips (assets/alta/parts: rise, dive, glide) play inside, shrunk to fit.

## The Dao, quietly

Found in things, never preached on signs.

- **Water, the teacher.** The water wheel turns because the stream flows (wu wei: doing without forcing). "Nothing is
  softer than water, yet nothing is better at wearing down the hard"; the labs sit by the water for that.
- **The Fish Bridge.** An arched footbridge over the stream to the labs (Zhuangzi, the joy of the fish). Stand on it a
  while and the fish come up.
- **The uncarved block (pu).** One plain block of wood on the workbench, never cut: everything it could still become.
  Tap it and it shows something it might become, different each time.
- **Cook Ding's knife.** A worn tool on the workshop wall, from the cook whose blade stayed sharp for nineteen years
  because he followed the grain.
- **The use of emptiness** (Laozi, chapter 11: thirty spokes share one hub; the empty centre makes the wheel useful).
  The light jars: empty glass that holds light. The empty hook on the key ring. One empty drawer in the Parts Depot,
  kept for what hasn't been made yet.
- **The Way is the trail.** "A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step." Small mile markers along the
  Field Notes trail count the real miles. Christian's motto on the first one, in his words: "I'm not here to break
  records. I just don't want to break anything."
- **Light and dark in turn.** The season picker as a small round dial, light and dark trading places as the year
  turns; day and night ease into each other.
- **The butterfly dream** (Zhuangzi). One butterfly drifts through the whole world; follow it to Daydreams.
- **The useless tree** (Zhuangzi). The tree nobody cut because its wood was "useless", so it grew huge and gives shade.
  The lake's big tree, with a bench under it.
