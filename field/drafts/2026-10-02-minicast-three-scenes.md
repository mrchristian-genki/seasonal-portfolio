# Mini-Cast draft: Three scenes and a sleeping dog

Status: draft, not published. Written Oct 2, 2026 from the day's work (both repos' commit logs and
the conversation). A "Mini-Cast" is a short indoor episode about making things, in the same
narrator voice as Field Notes (see SHOW-GUIDE.md: third person, write for the ear, only what
happened). Roughly 4 minutes at the show's pace.

Visuals to pair with it (all live): the canyon logo on the homepage; the deep-sea Parts Catalog
header; the Workshop header; the share cards for each.

## Script

It started with a small, practical problem. Christian wanted his website to look right when someone shared a link. Every site has a picture for that. His didn't.

***

So he went looking for one. He found an old illustration of himself and Marley at the edge of a canyon. Marley has the top of his hood in her mouth. Christian is hanging over the drop. It is, in its way, a rescue.

The plan was simple. Turn that picture into a little animation for the corner of the site. Rebuild it in layers, so the pieces could move.

The first attempt did not go to plan. With Marley lying down, the picture read less like a rescue and more like a dog chewing on a man. Christian put it plainly. She needs to be standing. She is saving me.

***

They kept the portrait in the end, close in, her chin on his hood. He sinks a little. His eyes go wide. She eases him back up, and his eyes relax. Not sleepy. Relaxed. That took a few tries.

Then came the seasons. Blossoms in spring, falling snow in winter, a moon and stars at night. The leaves in autumn were too much, so they went. The tiny picture in the corner now changed with the sky above the lake.

***

One logo made the next page look bare. The Parts Catalog, a library of every animal and tree the site can draw, still had a plain dot.

Christian had a new picture for it. Deep water. Marley curled up asleep inside a glowing jellyfish. Christian in an old brass diving helmet, holding out a small glowing cube of parts.

This time the pieces came separately. The diver. The dog. The jellyfish. The water. Then a handful of small sea creatures, each with its own soft light inside. An anglerfish got a lamp on its lure that flickers on every so often. The smaller creatures shrank to plankton. A few drift past in front, out of focus, the way things do underwater.

The title got a little water in it too. Two shades of blue, taken from inside the jellyfish.

***

Then the workshop. A page for building new parts deserved a place where things get built.

The question was old school or new school. They settled on both. A worn wooden bench, a brass lamp, pegboards of tools. And one thing from the future. A hologram.

The first version had Christian holding a soldering iron. Marley was asleep on the bench right in front of him. It looked, as he said, like he was soldering the dog. So the iron went. Now he wears goggles and shapes a glowing blue hologram with his hands. An eagle. Then an owl. Then a hawk.

His hair came out too red at first. It is sandy, not ginger. That got fixed.

Along the bench, a few of the site's own paper animals stand on small glowing discs. A doe. A mule deer buck. A black bear. And an elk bull, because, as Christian pointed out, an elk bull is massive. They all face him now, not the dog.

***

There was one last note. Marley, stretched out and very still at the end of the bench, could be read the wrong way.

So she got a slow, visible breath. And a little line of z's, drifting up from her head.

She is asleep. She is fine. She is, as usual, the best part of the picture.
