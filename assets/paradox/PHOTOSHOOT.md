# POPULAR PARADOX: the photoshoot, a prompt catalog

Pictures for the Tumble's feature, a lab-test review in a made-up 1970s–80s popular-science magazine. The test
pilot is GlazyArray, in her workshop goggles, at the controls of the time machine during an 88 event, and she is
NOT ready for it.

## Before every shot

- **Give your image tool two references:**
  1. **The cockpit:** `assets/paradox/ref-cockpit-88.jpg` (mid-88: the big dial and gauges glowing orange, the
     portholes blazing) or `assets/paradox/ref-cockpit-rest.jpg` (calm). Both are clean: no text, no logos, the
     jewels are plain coloured glass.
  2. **Her:** `assets/narrator/kit/her-reference.jpg`. Ask it to keep her exactly on-model.
- **Paste her description** (below) at the start of every prompt; it's marked [Her description.] in each one.
- **Never:** text, letters, numbers, logos, magazine names, real people or brands in the picture. The masthead,
  the 88 MPH and every label are added on the page, not in the render. The jewel lamps stay plain coloured glass.
- **Look:** a 1970s–80s popular-science magazine photo: punchy flash-lit colour, a little film grain, sharp, high
  drama, a sense of humour.

**Her description (in every prompt):**

> GlazyArray, a small rose-gold brass robot woman with a sculpted brass face and riveted seams, finger-wave brass
> curls that roll into side curls, lashes on her brass eyelids, calm Lake Tahoe blue eyes with warm creamy whites
> and small dark pupils, a little antenna topped with a glowing flower-bud bulb, glowing cyan glass tubes in her
> chest, and articulated brass hands. She wears brass workshop goggles with a leather strap and padded rims. Keep
> her exactly like the reference image.

## 1. The cover: "Hold on to your curls"

- **Size:** 4:5, at least 1600 × 2000 · **File:** `pp-cover.jpg` · **Goes:** the cover and the share card

> [Her description.] Magazine cover photo. She sits at the controls of the retro brass time-machine cockpit from the
> reference, facing the camera, both hands gripping the edge of the console, goggles pulled down over her eyes.
> Behind the lenses her eyes are huge and round with shock, her mouth a small round "o". Her curls are blown
> straight back as if by a sudden wind, her flower-bud bulb flaring bright. The big round dial behind her glows
> blazing orange, the portholes pour white-gold light, sparks drift in the air. Dramatic flash lighting, a little
> film grain. Clean dark space across the top third for a masthead. No text, no numbers, no logos.

## 2. The opener: the 88 moment

- **Size:** 16:9, at least 1920 × 1080 · **File:** `pp-88.jpg` · **Goes:** the opening spread

> [Her description.] Wide shot of the cockpit from the reference at the instant it hits 88: every gauge glowing
> orange, the big dial blazing, the portholes flaring white-gold, a shower of sparks. She is jolted back in her seat,
> both hands thrown up, goggles down, two streaks of fire reflected in the lenses, her curls whipping back. Motion
> blur on the sparks, everything else sharp. 1970s science-magazine action photo. No text, no numbers, no logos.

## 3. "Don't touch that knob"

- **Size:** 4:5 · **File:** `pp-knob.jpg` · **Goes:** the section on the knobs and the surge

> [Her description.] Close-up of her brass hand turning a round black knob on the console, a second knob already
> turned beside it. She looks sideways at the camera over the top of her goggles with a mischievous little grin, as
> if about to turn it a third time. Behind her the small gauges glow orange and a warm light swells from the
> portholes. Shallow depth of field. No text, no logos.

## 4. Golden bubbles

- **Size:** 4:5 · **File:** `pp-bubbles.jpg` · **Goes:** the section on the porthole

> [Her description.] She presses her goggles right up to a round brass porthole, her hands flat on the glass on
> either side, staring in wonder. Through the porthole, deep blue water and a rising rush of glowing golden bubbles,
> their light reflected warm on her brass face and in her lenses. Soft, magical, quiet. No text, no logos.

## 5. Hair-raising

- **Size:** 4:5 · **File:** `pp-static.jpg` · **Goes:** a pull-quote page

> [Her description.] Portrait at the controls during the power surge: her brass curls standing straight up on end
> with static, tiny arcs of light crackling around her antenna and flower-bud bulb, goggles pushed up on her
> forehead, eyes wide, lips pressed in a startled line, glancing sideways at the camera. Orange glow from the gauges
> below lights her face. Funny, dramatic, editorial. No text, no logos.

## 6. The test pilot's report

- **Size:** 4:5 · **File:** `pp-after.jpg` · **Goes:** the verdict, at the end

> [Her description.] After the jump: the cockpit calm again, a wisp of smoke curling up from her antenna bulb. Her
> brass face is dusted with soot except for two clean circles around her eyes where the goggles were, now pushed up
> on her forehead. She looks straight at the camera, utterly deadpan, holding a clipboard with blank pages against
> her chest. Soft morning light from the portholes. Dry comedy, editorial portrait. No text, no writing on the
> clipboard, no logos.

## 7. Optional: the cutaway

- **Size:** 16:9 · **File:** `pp-cutaway.jpg` · **Goes:** "How it works"

> A vintage 1970s popular-science cutaway illustration of the retro brass time-machine cockpit from the reference,
> drawn in gouache and ink: the console opened up to show glowing tubes, gears, coiled wires, the round dial's
> mechanism, a porthole with water and bubbles behind it, and a small brass robot pilot in goggles in the seat.
> Clean cream background, muted print colours, fine linework. No labels, no text, no numbers, no logos (the page adds
> the callouts).
