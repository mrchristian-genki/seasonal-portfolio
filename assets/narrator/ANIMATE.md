# RIVETING TONE, animated: a prompt catalog for living photos

Like a portrait in a storybook wizard's castle: each photo from her feature becomes a short, silent loop where
almost nothing moves. A blink, a glow, a lamp flicker, a lean. Each still stays the first frame, so the page looks
the same before the clip plays.

Each shot below has its file, how long it runs, what moves and what must stay still, and a prompt ready to copy.

## How to make one

1. **Start from the still.** Give your image-to-video tool the photo from
   `field/data/photos/2026-10-08-glazyarray-will-see-you-now/` (named below) as the first frame.
2. **Ask for a seamless loop.** If the tool has a last-frame or loop setting, set the last frame to the same still.
   If it doesn't, Claude can cross-fade the end back into the start.
3. **Keep it short:** 4 to 6 seconds, the photo's own size and shape, 24 or 30 fps, no sound.
4. **Bring it back** as an `.mp4` with the same name as the still (`desk.mp4` beside `desk.jpg`), in the Studio or
   to Claude. The Note already plays an `.mp4` photo as a muted loop, the cover too, with the still as its poster.

## Rules for every loop

- **Living photo, not a scene.** Tiny, slow, gentle motion. Most of the frame stays perfectly still.
- **The camera is locked:** no zoom, pan, orbit or shake. Background parallax is at most a whisper.
- **Her face stays on-model:** the same brass face, the same calm blue eyes with warm whites, and the lashes.
  - **Mouth:** closed, with the same soft smile. Her mouth only moves in her own videos, never here.
  - **Blinks:** at most one per loop, quick and natural, with both lids together.
- **Her hands keep their joints:** articulated brass fingers bend only at the joints, never melt or add a finger.
- **Glows breathe, they don't flash:** the cyan chest tubes and the flower-bud bulb pulse softly, about once every
  2 to 3 seconds.
- **Never:** text, logos, new people, new objects, a change of hat or hair, or anything appearing or disappearing.
- **The loop closes:** the last frame matches the first.

**Paste this at the start of every prompt:**

> A living photograph: a seamless, gently looping animation of this exact image. Locked-off camera, no zoom, no
> pan. Keep the robot GlazyArray exactly as she is: her brass face, her calm blue eyes, her closed soft smile,
> her hair and her hands. Subtle, slow, magical motion only, like an enchanted portrait. The last frame matches the
> first.

## Quick fixes when a loop goes wrong

- **Her face drifts or changes:** add "her face is frozen, only her eyelids move", or shorten the clip to 4 s.
- **The mouth opens:** add "her lips stay sealed in the same soft smile for the whole clip".
- **Too much motion:** add "90% of the frame is perfectly still", and remove one moving element.
- **The camera creeps:** add "tripod-locked, static shot, no camera movement of any kind".
- **The loop jumps:** set the last frame to the still, or send it to Claude to cross-fade the seam.
- **Extra fingers or melting hands:** keep the hands still in that shot, and move a glow instead.
