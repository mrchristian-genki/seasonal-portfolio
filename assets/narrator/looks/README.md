# GlazyArray's looks

Base layers for her head (623 × 437), made in the Workshop's Style Array; `looks.json` lists them.

She wears the holiday look coming up next (a look with `from`/`to`, month-day, is hers from the day after the
previous holiday ends through its own `to`). A click on her head moves her on through her wardrobe; the look picked
goes into the address (`look-name`) and the site's links carry it. `rotate` marks the hair styles. `?look-name` shows
one; `bulb: false` when a hat or bow covers the antenna.
