Lake scene trigger guide

Five rocks in the scene are secret triggers. Tap one and it glows, and an animal appears in the part of the scene that rock controls. Tap the same rock again to send the animal away. Press and hold any of them to open the test panel.

What each rock does

  1. Big rock, far left Far shore under the mountains

    Deer walking out of the trees and back in; doe drinking (spring to fall); elk (fall, winter); bear walking the shoreline (spring to fall, daytime); wolf running (fall, winter); wolf howling (fall and winter nights).

  2. Rock next to it Right bank with the three pines

    Deer stepping out from behind a pine trunk (a buck in winter); hawk on a pine top (spring to fall, daytime); owl on a pine top at night, and a white snowy owl in winter, day or night.

  3. Small rock beside the green bush Right bank

    Hare hopping along the right bank in front of the pines, white in winter.

  4. Big rock under the tree The lake

    Fisherman drifting across the lake and out of sight behind the right bank (spring to fall), with a lantern at night.

  5. Small rock above it Fox and chipmunk

    Fox trotting out from behind the big tree (or a right-bank pine) to look about and sniff, or a chipmunk popping up behind the small rock (chipmunk daytime, not in winter).

Every animal, and the word to test it

The note under each link says when that animal is about; the link opens a season where it shows. Its last word also works on its own, whatever the season: ?foreelk. Two are called up by touch instead: a fish jumps when you tap or hold the lake (spring, summer), and the eagle catches it when you hold the lake for about 2.5 seconds (spring to fall, by day; also “Eagle catches a fish” in the test panel). With a mouse, rings follow the pointer across the lake. The weather comes in three levels: rain is ?drizzle, ?shower, ?tempest (or ?storm); in winter snow is ?flurries, ?snow, ?blizzard. A storm also blows through now and then on its own. And somewhere on the lake is a way to a getaway: tap the fisherman’s paper boat (spring to fall) and it sails you to a tropical beach; ?beach goes straight there.

Scene effects that come on by themselves: fireflies at night (a different colour each season), Marley’s glowing collar at night, the lamp inside the tent at night, and wind gusts that start 5 seconds after a season settles.

Test panel

Press and hold any trigger rock for about a second, or add ?wildlife to the address. A small panel opens in the bottom-right corner listing every animal (use ⇄ to move it to the other side and – to fold it away): click one to play it, click again to send it away. “Any season / time of day” plays everything regardless of season; “Send everyone away” clears the scene.

On their own

About every 30 to 60 seconds one visitor arrives by itself, chosen to suit the season and whether it is day or night. The fisherman crosses the lake every two to three minutes in spring, summer and fall. Nothing plays while the scene is scrolled away, the tab is hidden, or a season change is running.
